About Ellen

Care planning built on clarity, empathy, and proven experience.

Ellen graduated from the University of Miami with a bachelor’s degree in organizational communication. As a mother of two and a dedicated long-term care specialist in Greensboro, NC, she brings unmatched clarity to one of life's most complex transitions.

Navigating long-term care can often feel overwhelming, emotionally heavy, and full of confusing insurance jargon. Ellen founded Long Term Care From Ellen with a single driving mission: to give families in Central North Carolina care guidance and financial security they can genuinely count on.

By combining her deep foundation in organizational communication with over two decades of local care advocacy, Ellen works alongside your family to design proactive protection—safeguarding hard-earned retirement assets, coordinating reputable local caregivers, and preserving dignity at every stage of life.

# Long Term Care From Ellen — Official Website

High-end, elegant standalone homepage for **Long Term Care From Ellen**, a premier long-term care planning and senior advocacy service based in Greensboro, North Carolina.

## 🎨 Branding & Color Palette
- **Sage Green**: `#A8B9A7` (RGB: 168, 185, 167)
- **Dusty Lavender**: `#AA8BAD` (RGB: 170, 139, 173)
- **Deep Plum**: `#370D3D` (RGB: 55, 13, 61)

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Full-Width Hero Section**: Immersive photography with deep plum gradient overlay and social proof badges.
- **Single-Line Header**: Sleek floating glass navigation with `white-space: nowrap` alignment.
- **Prominent Founder Showcase**: Featuring Ellen's authentic portraits across the Hero glass card, About section dual-portrait frame, and Step 04 satisfaction guarantee card.
- **Care Cost & Benefit Estimator**: Interactive glassmorphism calculator projecting North Carolina elder care costs vs. LTC insurance coverage benefits.
- **Complimentary Consultation Dialog**: Native HTML5 modal with glassmorphism backdrop blur and toast confirmation.
- **Accreditations**: Badges for AALTCI, National Care Planning Council, Greensboro Chamber of Commerce, Certified Senior Advisor, and NC Department of Insurance.

## 📂 File Structure
- `index.html`: Complete standalone responsive homepage (HTML5, modern CSS3, vanilla JS).
- `logo.jpg`: Official circular LTC from Ellen brand emblem.
- `ellen_portrait_1.jpg`: Ellen's formal business portrait.
- `ellen_portrait_2.png`: Ellen's personal smiling portrait.
